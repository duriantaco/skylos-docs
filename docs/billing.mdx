Skylos Cloud has three plans: **Free**, **Workspace** and **Enterprise**.

You pay once for a credit pack or the Workspace pass. Nothing renews. There are no subscriptions and no seats. Credits never expire.

The live price list is at [skylos.dev/pricing](https://skylos.dev/pricing).

:::tip No account needed
The CLI, the agent hooks and the PR gate in your own CI are free and need no account.
:::

## Free

Free costs $0 and needs no card. In Skylos Cloud it includes:

| Free | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Projects | 1 |
| Stored scans | Newest 10 per project |
| Pass/fail check on pull requests | Included |
| Issues marked "not fixing" | Up to 25 per project |
| Custom rules | Up to 3, no Python rules |
| Cloud scans | 5 a day |

Credits work on Free too. If the workspace has credits, every credit action works.

## Free Trial

An account's first workspace gets a **14-day Workspace trial** and **50 credits**.

- No card. Nothing renews or charges you.
- One trial per account and per GitHub user.
- On day 15 the workspace moves to Free. Settings and issues stay. Unused credits stay.

## The Workspace Plan

Any purchase turns on the Workspace plan for 12 months from that purchase. It adds:

- Up to 10 projects
- 500 stored scans per project
- 50 custom rules, Python rules included
- 50 Cloud scans a day
- Approvals and overrides
- Audit evidence and exports
- Teams, integrations and inline PR comments

### Buying Again During a Term

A purchase made while a term is running starts a new 12 months from that purchase. If you already hold a later end date, you keep it. Terms do not add up beyond that.

**Example:**
1. Buy a Starter pack on 1 January. Workspace runs until 1 January next year.
2. Buy a Builder pack on 1 June. Workspace now runs until 1 June the year after.
3. Credits from both packs add up in one balance.

### When the 12 Months End

- Nothing is charged.
- The workspace moves to Free.
- Settings and issues stay.
- New scans then keep the newest 10 per project.
- Your credits stay.

Buy any pack or the Workspace pass to turn Workspace on again.

Workspaces that bought before 14 October 2026 keep Workspace with no end date and no member limit.

## What Costs Credits

The app shows the cost before every charged action. Every plan pays the same costs, Enterprise included.

| Action | Credits | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Compare two scans | 2 | Charged only when the result is ready |
| AI fix PR (**Fix in PR**, or **Create cleanup PR** on a finding) | 3 | Refunded if it fails or the AI says the finding is a false positive |
| Weekly dead-code cleanup PR | 3 | Automatic, opt-in per project. Skipped when credits run out |
| MCP remediation (`remediate` tool) | 10 | Needs a project API key |
| Deep scan | 10 | Plus the model cost on your workspace's own Anthropic key. Refunded if no report. Shown only where deep scans are available |
| Compliance report | 500 | Once per framework and project selection. Downloads and retries are free |

## What Never Costs Credits

- Local CLI scans, in every language and output format
- Scan uploads (`skylos . --upload`), Claude Security uploads included
- Cloud scans of your GitHub repositories
- PR checks and the merge gate
- MCP scan tools (`analyze`, `security_scan`, `quality_check`, `secrets_scan`, `provenance_scan`). They need a project API key, except `analyze`, which allows 5 calls a day without one.

## When Credits Run Out

- A credit action is refused before it starts (HTTP 402, "Insufficient credits"). Nothing is charged.
- The button points to Billing.
- The weekly cleanup PR is skipped.
- Free actions keep working: uploads, Cloud scans, PR checks and the merge gate.

A CI upload never fails for lack of credits, because uploads and PR checks are free.

The MCP `remediate` tool returns:

```
Insufficient credits. Required: 10, available: 0. Buy credits at https://skylos.dev/dashboard/billing
```

## Refunds

- Cancel any purchase within 14 days. You get the price back, minus the value of the credits spent from it.
- One credit is worth the pack price divided by its credits. Example: you buy a Builder pack ($39, 250 credits) and spend 20 credits. The refund is $39 − 20 × $0.156 = $35.88.
- After 14 days, refunds are at Skylos's discretion.
- To ask, email `founder@skylos.dev` with your order number, or contact Lemon Squeezy.

Full terms: [skylos.dev/terms#payments](https://skylos.dev/terms#payments).

## How Purchasing Works

1. Open **Billing** at [skylos.dev/dashboard/billing](https://skylos.dev/dashboard/billing). `skylos credits` prints the link.
2. Pick a credit pack or the Workspace pass.
3. Pay on the Lemon Squeezy checkout page. Tax is added there.
4. Credits are added to the workspace, and the Workspace plan runs for 12 months from the purchase.

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No. They work on every plan, Free included.

**Does anything renew?**
No. Every purchase is a one-time payment. When the 12 months end, the workspace moves to Free and nothing is charged.

**What happens if my balance hits zero mid-CI-run?**
Nothing breaks. Uploads, PR checks and the merge gate never cost credits.

**Can I get a refund?**
Yes, within 14 days, minus the value of the credits you spent. See [Refunds](#refunds).

**What's the difference between plan limits and credits?**
The plan sets limits and features: projects, stored scans, custom rules, team features. Credits pay for compute actions, on any plan.
