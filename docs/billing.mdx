Skylos Cloud has three plans: **Free**, **Workspace** and **Enterprise**.
There are no subscriptions and no seats. You buy once, and nothing renews by
itself.

Credits pay for AI and compute actions. Any purchase also turns on the
Workspace plan for 12 months (see [How the Workspace plan works](#how-the-workspace-plan-works)).

The CLI, the agent hooks and the PR gate in your own CI are free, with no
account.

## New User Onboarding

Your first workspace gets a **Workspace trial**:

- **14 days** of the Workspace plan
- **50 credits** for compute actions such as scan comparisons, AI fix PRs and MCP remediation (scan uploads are free)
- No card, and nothing renews or charges you

The trial is given once per account and once per GitHub user. Later
workspaces start on Free with 0 credits.

On day 15 the workspace moves to Free. Settings and issues stay, and unused
credits stay yours. Credits work on every plan, Free included.

## What Costs Credits

Only compute actions in Skylos Cloud cost credits:

| Action | Credits |
|--------|---------|
| Scan comparison (diff between two scans) | 2 |
| AI fix PR | 3 |
| Weekly cleanup PR | 3 |
| MCP AI remediation (`remediate` tool) | 10 |
| Deep scan, where your workspace offers it (refunded if no report is made) | 10 |
| Compliance report | 500 |

The MCP `remediate` tool charges its credits before it runs, even with
`dry_run=True`.

## How The Workspace Plan Works

- Any purchase on Billing turns on the Workspace plan for 12 months from that purchase.
- If the workspace already has a later end date, it keeps the later one.
- Nothing renews by itself. There is no subscription to cancel.
- When the 12 months end, nothing is charged. The workspace moves to Free. Its settings and issues stay, and new scans keep the newest 10 per project.
- Your credits stay. Buy again to turn the Workspace plan back on.

Workspaces that bought before the 12-month terms keep their old terms.

:::tip
Credits never expire. Only the Workspace plan has an end date.
:::

## How Purchasing Works

1. Go to **skylos.dev/dashboard/billing** (or run `skylos credits` in the CLI to get the link).
2. Pick a credit pack or the Workspace pass.
3. You're redirected to a secure checkout page (powered by Lemon Squeezy).
4. After payment, credits are added to the workspace.
5. The Workspace plan is on for 12 months from the purchase.

## What Happens at Zero Credits

### Scan Uploads

```bash
skylos . --upload
```

Uploads keep working at zero credits. They never cost credits, so results are
still stored and your PR checks and merge gate keep working. Only compute
actions stop until you add credits.

### MCP Server

The `remediate` tool returns an error:

```
Insufficient credits. Required: 10, available: 0. Buy credits at https://skylos.dev/dashboard/billing
```

The scan tools (`analyze`, `security_scan`, `quality_check`, `secrets_scan`)
cost 0 credits, so they keep running.

### CI/CD

Same as CLI upload — uploads from CI are free, so a workspace with 0 credits keeps its PR checks and merge gate.

---

## Low Credit Warning

Uploads do not use credits, so a low balance never blocks them. The dashboard
marks the balance "Low" at 10 credits or fewer, and `skylos credits` prints
"Low credits!" below 10. Check your balance any time with `skylos credits` or
on the [billing page](https://skylos.dev/dashboard/billing).

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No. Credits never expire. Buy them once, use them whenever.

**Does the Workspace plan expire?**
Yes. Each purchase turns it on for 12 months. When it ends, the workspace moves to Free and keeps its credits. Buy again to turn it back on.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds follow the Lemon Squeezy checkout terms, the law, and any written agreement with Skylos. Contact `founder@skylos.dev`. A full refund removes that purchase's credits.

**What happens if my balance hits zero mid-CI-run?**
Nothing changes for the scan. Uploads never cost credits, so the upload, PR checks and merge gate keep working. Only compute actions, such as scan comparisons and AI fix PRs, stop until you add credits.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Your first workspace gets a 14-day Workspace trial with 50 credits, and no card. Local scanning is free with no limits.

**What's the difference between plan-gated and credit-gated?**
Plan features (longer history, team review, integrations, PR comments) need the Workspace plan but don't cost credits. Compute actions (scan comparisons, AI fix PRs, MCP remediation, compliance reports) cost credits on every plan, Free and Enterprise included. Scan uploads never cost credits.
