Skylos Cloud has four plans: **Free**, **Startup**, **Workspace** and
**Enterprise**. The CLI, the agent hooks and the PR gate in your own CI are
free, with no account. The team gate and the stack compiler are paid.
Startup and Workspace have a flat monthly or yearly price. Nothing is charged
by seat, line of code or pull request.

| Plan | Price | Credits included |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Buy packs when you need them |
| **Startup** | $125 a month or $1,500 a year | 41 a month or 500 a year |
| **Workspace** | $250 a month or $3,000 a year | 83 a month or 1,000 a year |
| **Enterprise** | Contact us, from $12,000 a year | 5,000 a year |

Startup includes everything in Workspace, for teams with up to 10
contributing developers: GitHub accounts, bots left out, that opened a pull
request in the workspace's connected repositories in the last 90 days. Where
these docs say the Workspace plan, Startup counts too.

What only the paid plans add for coding agents:

- **Skylos Compiler and Verified Stacks**: a large agent pull request becomes a stack of checked commits, and every layer of a GitHub stack is checked as it changes. See [Verified Stacks](/cloud#verified-stacks-and-the-skylos-compiler).
- **AI approval guard**: when an AI reviewer approves a risky pull request, its Skylos check waits for a person.
- **Done gate policy and history**: choose which agent checks block in every repository, and see each pull request's results. `skylos done` itself is free in the CLI.

Credits pay for AI fix PRs, cleanup PRs and compliance reports. Startup and
Workspace renew each month or year until you turn renewal off (see
[How Plans Renew](#how-plans-renew)).

Growth is no longer offered. Enterprise now includes its single sign-on,
extra projects and yearly invoice. Workspaces that bought Growth keep it.

## New User Onboarding

Your first workspace gets a **Workspace trial**:

- **14 days** of the Workspace plan
- **50 credits** for AI fix PRs and weekly cleanup PRs (scan uploads are free)
- No card, and nothing renews or charges you

The trial is given once per account and once per GitHub user. Later
workspaces start on Free with 0 credits.

On day 15 the workspace moves to Free. Settings and issues stay, and unused
credits stay yours. Credits work on every plan, Free included.

## How Plans Renew

- Startup and Workspace are paid by the month or by the year. Each payment keeps the plan on for that month or year and adds its credits.
- The plan renews at the same price. Billing shows the renewal date and price.
- Workspace owners get an email before each yearly renewal, with the date and price.
- You can turn renewal off at any time with **Cancel monthly renewal** or **Cancel yearly renewal** on Billing. Nothing more is charged, and the plan runs to the end of the month or year you paid for.
- A workspace has one renewing plan at a time. To move between monthly and yearly, turn renewal off on one before starting the other.
- When a plan ends, the workspace moves to Free. Its settings and issues stay, and new scans keep the newest 10 per project.
- Your credits stay.

Purchases made before 11 November 2026 keep the terms they were sold under.
A credit pack or Workspace pass bought before then still gives its 12 months
of the Workspace plan.

:::tip
Credits never expire. Only the plan has an end date.
:::

## How Purchasing Works

1. Go to **skylos.dev/dashboard/billing** (or run `skylos credits` in the CLI to get the link).
2. Pick Startup or Workspace, monthly or yearly, or a credit pack. A credit pack adds credits only; it does not turn a plan on. Startup can be bought while the workspace has 10 contributing developers or fewer.
3. You're redirected to a secure Stripe checkout. Stripe sells Skylos plans and credits as merchant of record, so your statement and receipts name Link.
4. After payment, credits are added to the workspace. A plan purchase also turns the plan on for the month or year.

If Billing says a plan's checkout is not open yet, or to start Enterprise or
the pilot, email `founder@skylos.dev`.

## What Happens at Zero Credits

### Scan Uploads

```bash
skylos . --upload
```

Uploads keep working at zero credits. They never cost credits, so results are
still stored and your PR checks and merge gate keep working. Only AI fix
PRs, cleanup PRs and compliance reports stop until you add credits.

### MCP Server

MCP tools cost 0 credits, so they keep running. That includes the scan tools
(`analyze`, `security_scan`, `quality_check`, `secrets_scan`) and `remediate`,
which runs the model on your own key.

### CI/CD

Same as CLI upload — uploads from CI are free, so a workspace with 0 credits keeps its PR checks and merge gate.

## Low Credit Warning

Uploads do not use credits, so a low balance never blocks them. The dashboard
marks the balance "Low" at 10 credits or fewer, and `skylos credits` prints
"Low credits!" below 10. Check your balance any time with `skylos credits` or
on the [billing page](https://skylos.dev/dashboard/billing).

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No. Credits never expire. Buy them once, use them whenever.

**Does the Workspace plan renew?**
Yes. Startup and Workspace renew each month or year, as chosen, until you turn renewal off on Billing. With renewal off, the plan runs to the end of the month or year you paid for, then the workspace moves to Free and keeps its credits.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds follow the checkout terms, the law, and any written agreement with Skylos. Contact `founder@skylos.dev`. A full refund removes that payment's credits and its month or year of the plan.

**What happens if my balance hits zero mid-CI-run?**
Nothing changes for the scan. Uploads never cost credits, so the upload, PR checks and merge gate keep working. Only AI fix PRs, cleanup PRs and compliance reports stop until you add credits.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Your first workspace gets a 14-day Workspace trial with 50 credits, and no card. Local scanning is free with no limits.

**What's the difference between plan-gated and credit-gated?**
Plan features (longer history, team review, integrations, PR comments, Verified Stacks, Done gate policy) need a paid plan but don't cost credits. Credit actions (AI fix PRs, weekly cleanup PRs, compliance reports) cost credits on every plan, Free and Enterprise included. Scan uploads, scan comparisons and MCP tools never cost credits.
